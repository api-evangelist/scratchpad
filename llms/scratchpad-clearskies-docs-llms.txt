# Clearskies

> Clearskies documentation for revenue teams and GTM Engineers. Learn how to connect your GTM stack — Salesforce, Gong, Scratchpad, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack — and unify the data into a Context Graph that gives Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, and Retool complete, always-current customer context via MCP. Covers the Clearskies agent builder, pre-built templates (deal risk monitor, post-call workflow, pre-call brief, AskAI), evals, adoption tracking, and deployment to Slack, Salesforce, and custom interfaces.

- [Workflows](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/overview.md): Automate revenue work with Clearskies workflows — a workflow runs on a trigger and moves through steps that find data, decide, and act, all on your Context Graph.
- [Triggers](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/triggers.md): Choose what starts a workflow — a meeting starting or ending, a recurring schedule, or a Salesforce record being created or changed.
- [Find & filter records](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/find-and-filter-records.md): Pull the accounts, contacts, deals, and more that a workflow should act on, and gate a run so it only continues when your conditions are met.
- [Send email from a workflow](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/actions/send-email.md): Add a Send Email or Draft Email step to a workflow — follow-ups, recaps, and alerts go out (or land in a draft) automatically, built from the data earlier steps produced.
- [Write to Salesforce from a workflow](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/actions/write-to-salesforce.md): Create and update Salesforce records as workflow steps — so a workflow doesn't just read your CRM and notify you, it keeps the record current.
- [Write to HubSpot from a workflow](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/actions/write-to-hubspot.md): Create and update HubSpot records as workflow steps — so a workflow keeps your CRM current instead of just reading from it.
- [Send a Slack message from a workflow](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/actions/send-slack-message.md): Post a message to Slack as a workflow step — recaps, alerts, and briefs land in the right channel or DM the moment the workflow runs.
- [Run an agent from a workflow](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/actions/run-an-agent.md): Add a Run Agent step so a workflow can reason over its data — summarize a call, draft a recap, classify a deal — and hand the result to the steps that follow.
- [Loops](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/loops.md): Repeat a step for every record in a set, so one workflow can act on many — a message per deal, a record per contact.
- [Human review on a workflow step](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/human-review.md): Pause a workflow for approval before an action runs — one reviewer decides whether the step goes ahead.
- [Workflow folders](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/folders.md): Organize your workflows into folders to keep a growing set of workflows manageable.
- [Manage workflows from Claude or ChatGPT](https://clearskies.cc/docs/workflows/manage-via-mcp.md): Create, edit, test, and publish Clearskies workflows from any AI you've connected to Clearskies, the full workflow lifecycle, without leaving the conversation.
- [Agents](https://clearskies.cc/docs/agents/overview.md): An agent is a reusable AI unit you build once and put to work — inside a workflow, and optionally behind its own command — all grounded in your Context Graph.

- [Manage agents from Claude or ChatGPT](https://clearskies.cc/docs/agents/manage-via-mcp.md): Build, read, update, and publish Clearskies agents from any AI you've connected to Clearskies — so you can create an agent in the same place you're already working.
