# Clearskies

> Clearskies is the context layer for revenue AI. It unifies fragmented revenue data and business context into one complete picture for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, n8n, and any AI workflow. Revenue teams use Clearskies to run CRO Briefs, pipeline risk workflows, account research, CRM hygiene, and deep revenue analysis.

## What it is

ChatGPT and Claude are powerful. But they don't know your customers. When your team asks about an account, a deal, or a pipeline risk, AI pulls from one source at a time — the CRM says one thing, the last call says another, and the Slack thread where the real conversation happened is invisible. Individual connectors give fragments. Clearskies unifies CRM, call transcripts, email, calendar, Slack, and support into a single Customer Context Graph — identities resolved, timelines built, gaps surfaced — and exposes it through one MCP endpoint, so any AI has the complete picture on every question.

## Key concepts

- **MCP server**: Clearskies is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server — the open standard Anthropic introduced for connecting AI to external data. Instead of wiring up Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Slack, and email one at a time inside Claude or ChatGPT, you connect them once to Clearskies and point any MCP-compatible AI at a single endpoint.
- **Customer Context Graph**: A unified, queryable layer that connects all customer data across systems with resolved identities, chronological timelines, and detected gaps.
- **Identity resolution**: Automatic matching of people, accounts, and deals across systems — sarah.kim@acme.com, Sarah Kim on a Gong call, and @sarah.kim in Slack are recognized as the same person.
- **Gap detection**: Identifies missing information and silent periods in customer engagement — champion went dark, deal went quiet, follow-up never sent.
- **Context layer**: Sits between your data sources and your AI. Connectors give access. Clearskies gives intelligence. The difference is whether AI guesses how your data connects, or already knows.
- **Single-tool MCP vs. unified MCP**: A single-tool MCP server gives AI access to one system in isolation. Clearskies resolves identity, timeline, and relationships across all connected systems before AI queries it — one coherent answer instead of five fragments the model has to stitch together. The benefit compounds: cheaper queries (one MCP call instead of 5–15), more consistent answers, portable to every AI your team uses, and one endpoint to deploy and govern.
- **CRO Briefs**: Daily or weekly executive revenue briefs for CEOs and CROs. CRO Briefs explain what changed, what matters, where risk is emerging, and which source evidence supports the readout. See https://clearskies.cc/cro-briefs.
- **Forward Deployed Revenue Engineers**: Clearskies implementation specialists who help revenue teams map workflows, connect systems and business context, build production AI workflows, tune quality, and hand off repeatable systems. See https://clearskies.cc/services/forward-deployed-revenue-engineers.

## What revenue teams are doing with it

- **CRO Briefs.** Daily or weekly executive revenue briefs covering pipeline movement, deal risk, competition, team patterns, customer health, and open questions — with source evidence from every connected system.
- **Pre-call prep.** Full briefing before every meeting: deal history, recent emails, Slack threads, past call highlights, open action items, in one view.
- **Pipeline risk detection.** Surface deals that have gone silent across all channels, not just stalled in the CRM. Champion dark 14 days, no email reply, no Slack activity.
- **Win-loss analysis.** Analyze every deal, won and lost, across every interaction. Surface the patterns: which competitors you lose to, where deals stall, what winning reps do differently.
- **Deal qualification.** Assess any deal against MEDDPICC, SPICED, or your own methodology, from actual calls, emails, and CRM data. Not what the rep said happened. What actually happened.
- **Rep coaching.** Coaching moments with timestamps from every call. Behavior patterns across deals. What top reps do differently, evidence-based.
- **CRM hygiene.** AI updates CRM fields automatically after every customer interaction, formatted exactly the way you define them. Based on actual calls and emails, not manual entry.
- **Account research.** Full account picture from every source, in one question. Cross-system, identity-resolved, timeline-ordered.
- **QBR prep.** Auto-generated QBR prep covering ARR, engagement trends, support history, risk signals, and expansion opportunities, from every source.
- **Competitive intelligence.** Every competitive mention across calls, emails, and Slack. Which competitors come up in which deals, what objections they raise, how your team responds.

## What the difference looks like

Without Clearskies: You ask Claude about the Acme deal. It pulls the Salesforce record — stage 3, $50K, close date next month. Looks fine.

With Clearskies: You ask the same question. Claude shows you the full picture — the champion hasn't responded to email in 12 days, the last Gong call surfaced a competing vendor, and a Slack thread from the AE flagged budget concerns. The deal isn't fine. It needs attention now.

Same AI. The difference is the context layer.

## How Clearskies compares

**vs. Gong:** Gong captures calls, email, and calendar — and locks it all inside Gong. You can't export it to power external AI. Clearskies makes all your customer data — including Gong calls — available to any AI you choose. No platform fee. No model lock-in.

**vs. Salesforce / HubSpot:** CRMs store structured records. They don't unify call transcripts, email threads, Slack conversations, or calendar context. Clearskies sits on top of your CRM and connects everything the CRM can't capture.

**vs. Salesforce Agentforce:** Agentforce is on-platform AI — it reasons over Salesforce data inside Salesforce. Clearskies is cross-stack: it resolves context across CRM, calls, email, Slack, and support, then serves it to any AI client. On-platform AI vs cross-stack AI. See https://clearskies.cc/vs/agentforce.

**vs. MCP connectors / building your own (connecting tools individually):** Five connectors give you five fragments. The AI still has to guess how they relate — and it gets it wrong often enough that you can't trust it. Clearskies resolves identity, builds timelines, and surfaces gaps before the AI asks a question. One connection instead of five, and no per-connector maintenance when APIs change. See https://clearskies.cc/vs/custom-mcp.

**vs. Clari / Salesloft:** Strong on forecasting and pipeline inspection, but model-locked and expensive. No conversation intelligence depth. No cross-source context graph.

**vs. Glean / enterprise AI search:** Glean searches your company's knowledge. Clearskies maintains your customer reality. Search finds what's there — Clearskies also sees what's missing. Gap detection (champion went dark, follow-up never sent) requires a pre-computed graph, not a search index. See https://clearskies.cc/vs/glean.

**vs. People.ai / Nektar.ai:** Activity capture and CRM hygiene from email and calendar. Narrower data surface — no call transcripts, no Slack, no cross-source graph, no gap detection.

**vs. data warehouses (Snowflake, Databricks):** Data warehouses store your data. Clearskies makes it AI-ready in real time. They work together — Clearskies reads from and writes to your warehouse.

**vs. hiring a consulting firm:** Traditional revenue consulting costs $100K–$300K and takes months, based on 25 calls and rep interviews. Clearskies analyzes every call, every deal, every interaction — in days. And the system keeps running after delivery.

**vs. dashboards / BI:** Dashboards show metrics. Clearskies-powered CRO Briefs synthesize the evidence behind the metrics: what changed, why it matters, which deals need attention, what is missing, and which source records support the readout.

**vs. generic AI implementation services:** Forward deployed revenue engineers build on the Clearskies context layer, so the work becomes a reusable operating workflow rather than a one-off prototype or static report.

## Security

Clearskies is built for governed AI workflows: permissions, security logging, and source-level controls follow existing customer data rules, so AI workflows do not create a shadow access layer. Clearskies is SOC 2 Type II compliant, and qualified customers and prospects can request SOC 2 Type II documentation and supporting security materials through the shared Scratchpad Trust Center: https://trust.scratchpad.com. Customer data is logically isolated per customer, encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, and stored on AWS in US-based infrastructure. Clearskies does not develop or train AI models; customer data is used to build and serve a governed revenue context layer for authorized workflows, not to train AI models. Data practices are aligned with GDPR and CCPA. A Data Processing Addendum is available at https://www.scratchpad.com/legal/data-processing-addendum. Subprocessor information is available at https://www.scratchpad.com/subprocessors. Security questions: security@clearskies.cc.
